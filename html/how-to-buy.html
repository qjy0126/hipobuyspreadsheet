How to buy

1. Find the item

Browse Findsheet categories or search. Verify the live Weidian / Taobao / 1688 listing.

2. Paste into your agent

Copy the source URL into Hipobuy (or CNFans, Kakobuy, Mulebuy, Sugargoo). Pay item cost.

3. Review warehouse QC

Inspect logos, shape, stitching and defects before you GL.

4. Ship internationally

Compare lines by weight and destination, pay freight, then track.
