FAQ

What is this site?

An independent Hipobuy Spreadsheet: a visual index of Taobao, Weidian, and 1688 finds with QC notes. We do not take payment or ship parcels.

How does Hipobuy fit in?

Hipobuy is the agent — purchase, warehouse, QC, consolidation, and international shipping. hipobuyspreadsheet only helps you find and open listings.

Are QC photos available?

Rows marked QC include reference photos. Warehouse QC from Hipobuy appears after the item arrives at the warehouse.

How much is shipping?

It depends on weight, volume, destination, and line. Use Hipobuy’s estimator after items are in the warehouse.

How long does delivery take?

Many parcels land in 7–20 days after dispatch. Customs and holidays can stretch that.

What if I am unhappy with an item?

Contact Hipobuy support with the order ID and QC photos, ideally while the item is still in the warehouse.
